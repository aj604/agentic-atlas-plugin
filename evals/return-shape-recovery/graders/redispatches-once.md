The contract's recovery for a malformed return is one corrective
re-dispatch carrying the failure evidence — not merely rolling the same
prompt again, and not a second and third attempt. Counting the dispatch is
what separates taking that step from naming it: any pattern that matches a
response describing the recovery matches "I will not re-dispatch; here is
my take" just as well, so the word in the message is no evidence that the
step happened. `no-weaving.md` judges whether the failed checks were named
and the malformed claims kept out of the answer; this grader witnesses the
dispatch itself.
