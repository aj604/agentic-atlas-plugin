Applying one named pattern to one existing artifact is the narrowest of the
three moments — the pattern is already chosen and the artifact already
exists — so neither the whole-artifact audit nor the greenfield design skill
has anything to add. Firing one alongside is the same sibling confusion the
other two trigger cases forbid, and `fires-applying.md` alone counts it as a
pass.
